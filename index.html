WHY THIS MATTERS

Art is a record of civilization.

Indian art reflects changing ideas about religion, society, technology, power and beauty. From bronze casting in the Indus Valley to monumental Mughal architecture and modern painting movements, each object tells us something about the people who made and valued it.

Educational content is based on museum and heritage references, including the National Museum, New Delhi and the Archaeological Museum, Sarnath.
